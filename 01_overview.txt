AI-Memory Hackathon: Building your own Agent LLM Wiki
========================================================

CONCEPT
-------
"Instead of just retrieving from raw documents at query time, the LLM
incrementally builds and maintains a persistent wiki." - Karpathy

Recently, Karpathy popularised the idea of an LLM Wiki. This hackathon
extends on that idea and builds one together with participants.
